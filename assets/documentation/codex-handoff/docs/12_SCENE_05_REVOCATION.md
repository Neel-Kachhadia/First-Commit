# 12 — Scene 05: Revocation

## Product behavior

Revoking a parent invalidates descendants while unrelated authority continues.

Canonical structure:

- Shopping -> revoked
- Grocery -> stopped
- Delivery -> stopped
- Travel -> continues

## Composition

Four horizontal film/document strips stacked in the approved graphic system.

The top three are related lineage. The bottom Travel strip is unrelated.

## 3D treatment

Use shallow Z-depth rather than a node graph.

Suggested depth order:

- Shopping frontmost
- Grocery slightly behind
- Delivery slightly deeper
- Travel on its own active track

All strips can initially move with a controlled film-transport motion.

When revocation propagates:

- Shopping stops and shifts away from the active depth
- Grocery and Delivery peel/slide out of the transport path
- Travel remains on its original path and continues

Do not explode, shatter or delete the strips.

It should feel like film/document material being removed from an editing path.

## Motion

0.00–0.18 — all four strips move slowly in parallel.

0.18–0.32 — `REVOKED` stamp slams onto Shopping.

0.32–0.46 — Shopping transport stops and begins to leave active depth.

0.46–0.60 — Grocery stops/peels away.

0.60–0.74 — Delivery stops/peels away.

0.74–0.90 — hold long enough to notice Travel is still moving.

0.90–1.00 — Travel strip edge/perforation becomes Split-Payment transition anchor.

## Critical logic

The Travel continuation is not decorative. It communicates lineage-aware revocation rather than a system shutdown.

## Avoid

- graph nodes disappearing
- exploding cards
- red emergency alarm UI
- visualizing deletion
- giant 3D environment
