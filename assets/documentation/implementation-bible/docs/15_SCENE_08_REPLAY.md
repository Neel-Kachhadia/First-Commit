# 15 — Scene 08: Causal Replay / Spatial Climax

## Product behavior

Reconstruct why a completed action happened through its causal chain.

Canonical layers:

- Mandate
- Delegation
- Agent Path
- Budget State
- Decision
- Execution
- Provider Result

The final transaction/outcome can sit beyond or in front of the last layer as a resolved result.

## Why this scene is special

This is the strongest marriage of the lost-film language and a real KavachPay capability.

It is also the **one scene where the site is allowed to become significantly more spatial**.

Spend the largest WebGL budget here.

Everything before this should feel restrained enough that Replay reads as a climax rather than just another 3D trick.

## Physical composition

Each causal layer is a thin physical strip/plane suspended through space.

Side-view concept:

```text
CAMERA →

[ MANDATE ]
      0.12 depth
            [ DELEGATION ]
                   0.15
                         [ AGENT PATH ]
                                0.12
                                      [ BUDGET STATE ]
                                             0.15
                                                   [ DECISION ]
                                                          0.14
                                                                [ EXECUTION ]
                                                                       0.16
                                                                             [ PROVIDER RESULT ]
```

The exact distances are tunable. The important thing is a readable depth rhythm.

## Front-facing art direction

Each plane must still look like the approved retro-print KavachPay system:

- off-white artifact surfaces
- black typographic hierarchy
- small pictograms
- film/strip edge language
- restrained red accents where semantically relevant

Do not turn the scene into holographic data panels.

## Forward traversal

0.00–0.12 — winning Concurrency receipt extends into the Replay stage.

0.12–0.22 — camera finds the Mandate layer.

0.22–0.34 — move through Delegation.

0.34–0.46 — Agent Path.

0.46–0.57 — Budget State.

0.57–0.68 — Decision.

0.68–0.79 — Execution.

0.79–0.88 — Provider Result.

Current layer sharpens; previous layers darken or soften slightly.

Important labels can remain DOM/SVG overlays for clarity.

## Reverse reconstruction

0.88–0.92 — brief hold on completed result.

0.92 onward — camera reverses and reconstructs why it happened.

On the return pass, reveal information that was not emphasized during the forward pass:

- exact mandate constraint
- delegated source
- budget state at decision time
- policy result
- provider correlation

The return pass should feel like **causal understanding**, not just reversing footage.

## Signature spatial move

At one camera position, all planes should briefly align into a coherent flattened stack/composition.

For a moment, the entire causal chain becomes one readable image.

Then as the camera continues, the layers separate again.

This is the scene's memorable spatial idea.

## Depth of field

Optional here only.

If performance allows, use limited DOF to move attention from layer to layer.

If not, fake focus with material opacity/contrast or preblurred variants.

Product readability always wins over optical effect.

## Reverse scroll

This scene must work perfectly backwards.

Timeline progress is the only source of truth.

No irreversible scene state.

## Transition to finale

After the reverse reconstruction, the physical layers compress toward camera alignment.

Then:

- film/strip edges collapse into a thin ivory rule
- WebGL stage fades/releases
- hard cut to black
- same rule redraws beneath final KavachPay wordmark

## Avoid

- flying through a sci-fi data tunnel
- glowing panels
- floating glass cards
- deep architectural environment
- literal database graph
- excessive post-processing
